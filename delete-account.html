Delete your WorkZone account

WorkZone - Field Management is operated by Janis. This page explains how to delete your account and associated data.

Delete your account in the app (recommended)

  1. Open WorkZone and sign in.
  2. Open Settings (gear icon).
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete My Profile.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete.

Your profile and company membership are removed from our database, and you are signed out immediately.

Company owners

If you own a company, you can tap Delete Company in the same Danger Zone section of Settings. This permanently deletes the company, its work areas, and related company data. Other members become individual users and keep their own accounts.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email janisd.dev@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Use the subject line WorkZone Account Deletion Request.

We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days, or within the timeframe required by applicable law.

What is deleted

When you delete your profile, we delete or disassociate:

  • Your name, email address, and profile information
  • Your company membership, if applicable
  • Work areas you created (individual accounts)
  • Your assignments on shared work areas
  • Account and session data needed to use the app

What may be kept

We may retain limited data when required by law or for legitimate purposes, such as:

  • Backup copies for a short period until overwritten
  • Aggregated or anonymized data that no longer identifies you
  • Records we must keep for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons

Retention period

After account deletion, personal data is removed from active systems promptly. Residual copies in backups may be retained for up to 12 months, unless a longer period is required by law.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual work areas from the map or work list without deleting your account.

For other partial deletion requests, contact janisd.dev@gmail.com.
