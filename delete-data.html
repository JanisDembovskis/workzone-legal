Delete your WorkZone data

WorkZone - Field Management is operated by Janis. Use this page to delete specific data without deleting your account.

Delete a work area in the app

  1. Open WorkZone and sign in.
  2. Open the work area on the map or from the work list.
  3. Open the area details panel.
  4. Tap Delete (available if you created the area or have manager/owner permissions).
  5. Confirm the deletion.

This permanently removes that work area and its associated data, such as descriptions, hazard zones, assignments, completion notes, and completion photos linked to that area.

Company managers and owners

Depending on your role, you can also delete company-related data without closing your account:

  • Delete a branch from Company settings (removes branch assignments).
  • Delete company work areas you manage from the map or work list.
  • Delete the entire company from Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Company (removes company data; your personal account remains unless you also delete your profile).

Request deletion by email

To request deletion of other specific data, email janisd.dev@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Use the subject line WorkZone Data Deletion Request and describe what you want removed.

We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days, or within the timeframe required by applicable law.

What is deleted

When you delete specific content in the app, we delete or remove:

  • Work area names, descriptions, and coordinates
  • Hazard zones and completion status for that area
  • Worker assignments and completion notes for that area
  • Completion photos uploaded for that area
  • Branch records you delete (including related assignments)

What is kept

Partial deletion does not delete your account. We keep:

  • Your profile (name, email, role, and account credentials)
  • Other work areas, branches, or company data you did not delete
  • Company membership, unless you leave or are removed separately

We may also retain limited backup copies until overwritten, or records required by law.

Retention period

Deleted content is removed from active systems promptly. Residual copies in backups may be retained for up to 12 months, unless a longer period is required by law.
